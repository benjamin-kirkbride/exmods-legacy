The Air Blower is the steam-driven route to blast pressure, bolted to a Cornish or a Watt engine
as its sub-machine and taking its throttle setting rather than a control of its own. Its own left
face, turned to face the way the engine sits, is the pipe connector itself: run a pipe up to that
face rather than in front of it, which is the opposite of how the twin-tub blower wants its, since
that one takes its pipe a cell beyond its own outlet. See [[Ironmaking]] for the flow each engine
and throttle setting gives it and [[Bessemer
process]] for why this is the only route to the pressure steel needs.
