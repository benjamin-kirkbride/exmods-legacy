---
title: Molten Canal (T-Junction), cobblestone
hints: [blockhelp-canal-clearsolidified]
---

A three-way split in a canal run, holding up to 100 units. It fills whichever of its open exits is
not already full first, not the one you meant, so an unwanted branch has to be sealed or left
unbuilt rather than trusted to stay empty. Built from cobblestone, cut from whatever rock the site provides. See [[Molten metal]] for sealing a branch and
how metal chooses its path at a junction.
