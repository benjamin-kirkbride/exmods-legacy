A Molten Canal (Tap) is a canal cell with a drain on it: park a barrel or one of the two large
molds underneath with a sneak right-click, then sprint right-click to toggle the pour, which
drains at 20 units per second. Closing the pour severs the canal at this cell, so nothing backs up
behind a shut tap. It is the fitting to use for the anvil and helve hammer molds, which are too
large for a pedestal. See [[Casting]] for filling a mold this way and [[Molten metal]] for the run
feeding it.
