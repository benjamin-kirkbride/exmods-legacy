---
title: Molten Canal (X-Junction), brick
hints: [blockhelp-canal-clearsolidified]
---

A four-way split in a canal run, holding up to 100 units. Like the T-junction it fills its first
open exit rather than sharing evenly, so a run with more than one destination needs the branches
you do not want fed sealed off. Built from fired brick, in the same colours a kiln turns out. See [[Molten metal]] for how metal is shared, or not,
between a junction's exits.
