---
title: Molten Canal (Straight), brick
hints: [blockhelp-canal-seal, blockhelp-canal-unseal, blockhelp-canal-clearsolidified]
---

A plain run segment, carrying up to 100 units and passing metal on toward whatever the run's start
feeds it. An empty straight is also the only shape that seals: right-click it holding at least 4
fire clay to cap it into a manual valve, severing the network there, and a chisel breaks the seal
open again, giving 2 fire clay back. Built from fired brick, in the same colours a kiln turns out. See [[Molten metal]] for how a cell freezes and what it takes
to chip one clear.
