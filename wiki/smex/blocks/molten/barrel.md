The Molten Barrel is bulk storage and a heat sink rather than a dispenser: park one under a Molten
Canal (Tap), which fills it at 20 units per second, and it holds up to 800 units, remelting
anything already set inside when fresh metal lands on it. Metal never flows back out of a barrel
into the canals; the only way to get it back is to let it set and chisel it out, in hand with a
hammer in the off-hand, at the usual 5 units per bit. Breaking a barrel that is both full and
hardened returns only the empty barrel, so drain or chisel it first. See [[Molten metal]] for the
canal side of feeding one.
