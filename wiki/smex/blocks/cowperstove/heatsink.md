Four Heat Sinks stacked in a stove's middle column are the whole of what stores its heat: look at
one to read the core's current temperature. They take exhaust's heat while the stove is charging and
give it back to the air while it is blowing, and a stove built without all four will not complete at
all. Each has to face the same way as the stove's intake, or the stove stays incomplete; a wrench
turns one a quarter turn in place, left click one way and right click the other, and it keeps its
heat. See [[Hot blast]] for the charge and discharge rates and why a charged stove cannot be banked
for later.
