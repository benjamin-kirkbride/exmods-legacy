Four Heat Sinks stacked in a stove's middle column are the whole of what stores its heat: look at
one to read the core's current temperature. They take exhaust's heat while the stove is charging
and give it back to the air while it is blowing, and a stove built without all four will not
complete at all. See [[Hot blast]] for the charge and discharge rates and why a charged stove
cannot be banked for later.
