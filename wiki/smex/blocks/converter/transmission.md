The Bessemer Transmission sits directly under the [[Bessemer Control]] and is where an axle drives
the whole converter: the vessel loads the shaft by 0.25 once it is turning above 0.1 geared speed.
It has to face the same way as the control block, or the structure stays incomplete and the build
outline marks the transmission in red. A wrench turns it a quarter turn in place, left click one way
and right click the other, and it couples to the axle on the side it then faces. See [[Bessemer
process]] for what the vessel does with that power once it has it.
