The Reinforced Hopper is the furnace's magazine: right-click to open it and load its eight slots,
four for iron feed, two for fuel and two for the lime flux. It combines them into burden a batch
at a time and hands the result down to the [[Bell Hopper]] beneath it, which is what actually
drips it into the shaft. Ctrl and right-click toggles the whole feed on and off; a hopper switched
off keeps whatever it is holding. See [[Ironmaking]] for the batch sizes and which feed the hopper
spends first.
