The Bell Hopper is the drip feed between the Reinforced Hopper above it and the furnace shaft
below: it buffers up to 48 units of burden and lets 4 units per second fall while dropping is
switched on. It has no controls of its own; ctrl and right-click on the Reinforced Hopper above it
starts and stops the whole feed, and the Bell Hopper simply keeps the shaft topped up while there
is anything to drop. See [[Ironmaking]] for how burden is made and what a full shaft means.
