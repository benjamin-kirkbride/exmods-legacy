The Twin-Tub Blower is the mechanical alternative to a steam Air Blower: an axle turning its
frame drives the two tubs the stages raise, and it pushes whatever that turning is worth straight
into the pipe fitted at the front. It never needs a boiler, but it also never clears the pressure
the [[Bessemer process]] wants; a waterwheel or a windmill through it makes iron, not steel. See
[[Ironmaking]] for the speed-to-flow curve and the 2.0 atm seal it cannot pass.

Feed it power at the axle cell above the frame and run a pipe up to the outlet cell in front of
it, facing back at the blower. A pipe merely resting nearby, with no connector facing the outlet,
carries nothing away, which is why a built and turning blower can still leave the furnace reading
zero blast.
