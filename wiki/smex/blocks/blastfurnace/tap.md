The furnace carries two of these, one draining the lower, iron reservoir and one the upper, slag
reservoir, each at 40 units per second. A tap only opens over a [[Molten Canal (Start), brick]] (or
the cobblestone kind) placed one block below its spout; toggle it with an empty hand, and it refuses
with nothing under it to drain into. Each tap has to face the way the furnace's layout sets for its
wall; turned any other way it leaves the furnace incomplete, and the build outline marks it red. A
wrench turns it a quarter turn in place, left click one way and right click the other, and an open
tap stays open. See [[Ironmaking]] for the reservoir sizes and [[Molten metal]] for laying out the
run it feeds.
