The furnace carries two of these, one draining the lower, iron reservoir and one the upper, slag
reservoir, each at 40 units per second. A tap only opens over a [[Molten Canal (Start), brick]]
(or the cobblestone kind) placed one block below its spout; toggle it with an empty hand, and it
refuses with nothing under it to drain
into. See [[Ironmaking]] for the reservoir sizes and [[Molten metal]] for laying out the run it
feeds.
