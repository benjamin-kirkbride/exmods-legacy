This is the mold before it is fired: clay-form it like any vanilla tool mold, in plate, double
ingot or quad rod shape, then bake it in a pit or a kiln to get the [[Ceramic mold, fired]] that
actually casts metal. The colour it comes out as depends on how far through the firing it was
when it finished and carries no meaning of its own; every colour of a given shape fires and casts
the same. See [[Casting]] for what each shape yields and how to switch a mold off server-wide.
