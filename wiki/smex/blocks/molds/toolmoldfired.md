The fired mold is what actually casts metal: set it on a Molten Canal (Mold Pedestal) and open the
pour to fill it, then take the casting out once it reads full and hardened. It comes from firing
the [[Ceramic mold, raw]] in a pit or kiln; colour is left over from that firing and does not
change what the mold makes. See [[Casting]] for how much each shape holds, what it yields, and the
rules for carrying a mold that is still hot or still liquid.
