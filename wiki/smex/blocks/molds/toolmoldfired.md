---
title: Ceramic molds, fired
---

This is what actually casts metal: set it on a [[Molten Canal (Mold Pedestal), brick]] (or the
cobblestone kind) and open the pour to fill it, then take the casting out once it reads full and
hardened. The three shapes are the plate, the double ingot and the quad rod, holding 200, 200 and
400 units respectively. It comes from firing [[Ceramic molds, raw]] in a pit or kiln; colour is
left over from that firing and does not change what the mold makes. See [[Casting]] for what each
shape yields and the rules for carrying a mold that is still hot or still liquid.
