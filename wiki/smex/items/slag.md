The recovered form of the furnace's slag: chip it from a frozen canal cell or a set Molten Barrel
with a chisel and hammer, at 5 units per bit, the same rate as every other metal recovery in the
mod. Grind it into [[Powdered Slag]] for fertilizer and mortar, or pave with it directly as [[Slag
Path]]. See [[Slag and its products]] for where it comes from and everything it can become.
