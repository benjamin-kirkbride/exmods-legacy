This is the sub-machine, not the engine: it seats in an engine's drive cell and turns the axle
network out of its own gear housing. Its budget is twice the engine's current power, and the shaft
settles wherever that budget divided by the load on the line lands: at the budget it turns at rated
speed, at twice the budget it labours at half speed, and at four times the budget it stalls
outright with no damage done.

Speed has a second ceiling on top of that: the shaft never turns faster than the engine driving it
turns, so a second engine on the same line buys load capacity and never buys speed.
