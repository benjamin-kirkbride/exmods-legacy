A 90 degree turn on the same masonry-cast pipe as Pipe Passthrough (Straight), for a run that has
to change direction while staying buried in a wall or a floor, such as the water line that turns up
under a boiler's block from underneath its firebox floor. It is exempt from over-pressure failure
and never caps a run's rating.

Get its turn wrong and a wrench will only cycle the orientations valid in its cell; where just one
fits, break it, build the neighbour that decides its shape, and place it again.
