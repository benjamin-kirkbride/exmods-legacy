A 90 degree turn in a run, iron or steel, oriented to its neighbours as you place it and turned
with a wrench afterwards. It is one of the four shapes that take part in over-pressure failure: the
weakest plain pipe on a run, this one included, sets the burst rating for the whole thing, 5 atm
for iron and 10 for steel, so one iron bend in an otherwise steel line caps it.

A wrench cycles only the orientations valid in the cell it sits in. Where only one is valid it will
not turn at all: break it, place the neighbour that decides its shape, and place it again.
