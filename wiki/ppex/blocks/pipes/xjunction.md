A four-way crossing, iron or steel, orienting itself to its neighbours as you place it. It is one
of the four shapes that take part in over-pressure failure, so the weakest plain pipe on a run,
whether or not it is this junction, sets the burst rating for the whole thing.

A wrench cycles only the orientations valid in its cell; where the neighbours already decide it,
there is nothing to cycle and it will not turn.
