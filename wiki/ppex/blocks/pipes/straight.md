The plain pipe segment a run is built from, iron or steel, orienting itself to its neighbours as
you place it. It is one of the four shapes that take part in over-pressure failure: one pipe holds
30 L at 1 atm, and the weakest plain pipe anywhere on a run, this one included, sets the burst
rating for the whole run, 5 atm for iron and 10 for steel.

Hold that ceiling for 30 s with nowhere to vent and one pipe at the rating, chosen at random, lets
go: its cell turns to air and the run splits in two at the gap.
