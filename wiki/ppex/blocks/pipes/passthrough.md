The same straight pipe as Piping (Straight), cast into masonry so a wall or a floor can cross a
run without a gap in it: the boiler fireboxes and the furnace walls of Steelmaking Expanded are
built from these. It is exempt from over-pressure failure and never caps a run's rating, whatever
pressure the run reaches.

Stand a chimney on its open top connector to draw 16 L/s of gas through it instead of leaking
there as an open end, the way a boiler's firebox is exhausted and given its draught.
