The crafted block is the firebox end of the machine: place it where the firebox is to stand, and
the vessel is raised from the cells in front of it. Ctrl and shift and right-click outlines the
cells still missing; a plain right-click with a stage's materials in the hotbar raises the next
stage. Its look-at line reports the whole vessel, water, steam, pressure and fire, and breaking it
takes the construction down and returns this block with all of the materials.
