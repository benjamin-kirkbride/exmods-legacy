The crank stands on one face and the delivery leaves the opposite one: place it with the crank
side toward you and the water comes out the far side, straight into the line you are feeding.
Holding right-click cranks it by hand at 2 L/s and a fixed 1 atm of head, low enough that it
flashes nothing when it feeds a boiling vessel.

It moves water, it does not make any: a Fluid Intake with a full water cube has to sit on the same
network as the crank's source face, or the pump runs and delivers nothing.
